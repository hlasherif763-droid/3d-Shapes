What is a Net? (Page 68)

A net is a flat 2D pattern of shapes that you can fold along its edges to make a 3D shape!

🧊 Closed Cube
Has 6 square faces. Completely closed on all sides like a dice!
📦 Open Cube
Has only 5 square faces. Missing 1 face on top like an open box!
